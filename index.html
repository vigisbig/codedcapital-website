How it works

From signal to trade, automatically

CodedCapital watches the market for you, fires signals when conditions are met, and executes trades according to your rules.

1

Configure your strategy

Pick your strategy, set your risk parameters — stop-loss levels, position size, leverage — and save it as a named config, that you can switch in and out anytime.

2

Market scans & signals

The platform continuously scans NSE stocks across timeframes. When a qualifying signal appears, it queues the trade for execution.

3

Automated entry & exit

Orders are placed, stop-losses trail automatically, and positions are squared off when your targets or risk limits are hit — with or without you at the screen.
